## Key excerpts (verified from PDF text)

- 2407.21086: "With step size ∆t = 0.2, the glider persists for 100 update steps, but disappears earlier for step sizes ∆t = 0.17 or 0.26." — engine-conditioned form survival; title literally "Non-Platonic".
- 2402.10236: individual defined as "ability of a self-organizing structure to preserve and propagate some spatiotemporal unity… making it a distinguishable coherent entity" + multi-entity runs show agents "preserve their individuality" and show attraction/reproduction — an entity ecology in CA, qualitative.
- 2512.09352: Γ "quantifying structural persistence under component turnover… bimodal distribution… phase transition… 1.77-fold variance collapse… 'structural zombie' systems in which sustained activity masks architectural decay."
- 2607.00220: identity via "admissible lifecycle paths… trustworthiness-level-preserving transformations… natural isomorphism witnessed by time-synchronous comparison morphisms" — transformation-invariance definition of diachronic identity.
- 2005.03742: "only the Orbidae family (out of 18 families) engages in… collisions… reflect each other and survive, or occasionally stick together to form a composite soliton Synorbium" — species-level entity interaction taxonomy.

## Positioning verdict

No paper we found does what E1/E2/B1/B2 do: **quantitative entity demographics (births,
lifespan distributions, leader-share entropy) + an executable identity-transplant test
(B1) + a measured identity radius (B2)** inside one engine. Closest neighbors are the
Lenia/CA-agency community (phenomenology, no identity tests) and the AI-metaphysics
community (identity criteria, no simulations). That gap is our publishable wedge.
