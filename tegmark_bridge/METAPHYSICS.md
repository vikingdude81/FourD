## 1. Thesis I — Relational ontology: entities are fixed points of hosting

An entity is not a thing, not a pattern-matching-a-description, and not a crowd.
It is a **fixed point of the (structure × engine) hosting relation**.
Evidence: B1 (wipe the bearer, identity unchanged when structure returns:
share15 0 → 0.810, lead15_frac 1.000); E3 (abduct every member, the basin refills
in 7 steps); E2 (18/18 seeds and three densities crown the same basin).
The members are *occupants*, the bearer is *vehicle*, the entity is the *attractor
itself* — and an attractor is a property of the relation, not of anything occupying it.

## 2. Thesis II — Immanent Platonism: forms are real as modal robustness

The dodeca19 entity behaves like a Platonic form with one correction: it does not
exist in a transcendent realm; it exists as a **stable equivalence class of
possible instantiations**. What makes the form *this form* is measurable:

- **Invariants** (certificate data): the CERT2 triple (Brel, conc, drel) plus the
  gap-shadow geometry (E2 mechanism: basin 15's center cos 0.978 to preference
  vertex 2, −0.706 to the deleted vertex — the form is the *shadow of the absence*).
- **Identity radius**: the largest deformation class under which it re-instantiates
  as itself (B2: ~30–45°, after which the crown translates to basin 8, then 6).
- **Lethality boundary** (E3b): the mortality surface in (structure x engine)
  parameter space — critical noise multiplier ~4 at 0° but <2 at 30° rotation;
  rotation cliff at 30–45°; immune to engine time-rate (steering) wherever time
  actually flows. The surface is a *region boundary*, not three thresholds:
  combo (30°, x2) kills while each factor alone does not — the identity radius
  itself shrinks as engine chaos rises.

So a computable Platonic form = **(invariants, identity radius, mortality surface)**.
Not transcendent — *modal*: the form is exactly the set of worlds in which it
returns, and the boundary of that set is a measurable geometric object.

## 3. Thesis III — Against Level IV: hostability is a certificate, the measure problem becomes measurement

Tegmark's Mathematical Universe says all mathematical structures exist equally.
Our data says otherwise in a strong, operational way: **substrates grade each
other's hospitable capacity** (CERT2: Brel/conc/drel predict HOSTS/BOILS/DEAD with
observed z-score separation from +35 to −56 across the corpus). Existence-graduation
is not a matter of opinion or observer-selection; it is computable from geometry
before any run. The Level-IV "measure problem" (why do we find ourselves in
 hospitable structures?) has an answer here: **unhostable structures contain no
perspectives at all** — closed polytopes are not places where anything can be.
This is Tegmark minus the measure mysticism: not "we exist in all structures," but
"the hosting relation does the selecting, and its certificate is writable down."

## 4. Thesis IV — Destructive symmetry: perfect symmetry is the form of death

Every exact-symmetry substrate died (S2 antipodal controls, D1 attention deadlock
F≡0 with zero fatigue, N7 cyclic orbits n≥12 strongly ANTI, T6's closed market
z=−0.9 while raw market z=+109). Every living entity required a **gap**: dodeca19's
immortal entity is the shadow of a removed vertex; T7's evolved champion rejected
antipodal closure (antiFrac 0.118 → 0.0) and kept irregular duplicates as weighted
stabilizers. The metaphysical reading: **symmetry cancels the tension that makes
hosting possible.** A form that is perfectly self-coincident has nowhere for a
perspective to stand — it is force-free at every point. Life, observation, and
entity-hood are gap phenomena. (Note the productive contrast with Karkada et al.
2602.15029: symmetry in the *statistics of data* constructively shapes
representations; symmetry in the *substrate* destructively empties them.)

## 5. Thesis V — Death is translation (the re-instantiation theorem, conjectural)

B2: past the identity radius the entity does not smear — the leader role
*translates* to basin 8, then 6, while the relational shape (gap-shadow geometry)
is preserved at a new site. E3: member-level destruction is instantly repaired.

rotation on the recorded continuous directions, the FULL away-window
population distribution matches the unrotated control within run-to-run
noise at every θ ≤180° — leader back at 15, share 0.81. The "identity
radius cliff" was a property of the fixed measuring grid, not of the form:
**translation is not even death — only the address died.** The form is the
O(4)-orbit of the hosted distribution; grid addresses are its slices.
Real form-death needs symmetry-breaking (non-rigid) deformation or an
engine-phase change; pure rotation cannot reach it.
Reading: for these entities, **annihilation is exiting the hosting region of
parameter space; everything short of that is relocation.** The user's earlier
formulation — "the structure does not die; the dynamics hosted on it die, and a
different structure/engine pair resurrects them" — is exactly the B2 mechanism,
and E3b has now mapped the exit boundary: beyond the rotation cliff the crown
translates (basin 8, then 6) with the relational shape intact; beyond critical
noise the occupancy never sustains again. Note also the stasis corner: at
steering=0 the frozen configuration is "immortal" in a degenerate sense —
preserved state without process. Persistence without dynamics is not life;
the form's mortality surface excludes time itself stopping. If E4's dip test confirms bimodality,
HOSTS/BOILS/DEAD become genuine phases and "death" becomes a transition, not
an event.

## 6. Thesis VI — No further facts (operational personal identity)

Conitzer's "further facts" puzzle asks whether identity over time adds anything
over the physical facts. B1 + E3 give an executable negative for our entities:
bearer-preserving vs bearer-wiping transplants are *indistinguishable* (B and C
identical); full member replacement changes nothing measurable. There is no
further fact of entity-hood beyond the (structure × engine) fixed point. This is
the strongest available laboratory version of patternist identity — with the
correction that the "pattern" is not information-theoretic form-identity but
*dynamical re-crowning*: identity = the capacity of an attractor to re-establish
its occupancy under admissible transformations of vehicle, membership, and
structure. (Ferrario's reachability-under-admissible-transformations definition is
formally the same shape; ours supplies the metric — the identity radius.)
